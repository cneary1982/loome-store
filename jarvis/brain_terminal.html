Walk-Forward Lab

Backtesting

Upload historical price data (OHLCV), then test any strategy against it. Walk-forward means the strategy only sees past bars — never peeks ahead. The HOLDOUT verdict is the only number you can trust — it's the final chunk of data the strategy never trained on.

Why backtest? To know if a strategy actually works before risking real money. If the holdout win rate and avg R look good, it's worth deploying live.
